# B2B Sales Funnel & Revenue Analytics

## Project Overview
This project analyses a synthetic B2B sales dataset containing 1,000 leads across different industries, products, cities, and lead sources.

The objective is to understand lead performance, sales outcomes, revenue trends, and reasons for lost deals using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

## Tools Used
- **SQL (SQLite):** Data validation, analysis, aggregations, and business queries.
- **Python:** Data analysis and chart generation using Pandas and Matplotlib.
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and business performance visualisation.

## Dataset
- 1,000 lead records
- 15 columns
- Includes lead source, industry, product, sales status, deal value, actual revenue, and follow-up dates.
- The dataset is synthetic and created for portfolio and learning purposes.

## Key Findings
- Total leads analysed: 1,000
- Won deals: 125
- Win rate: 12.5%
- Lost deals: 135
- Actual revenue: ₹10.30 crore
- Total deal value across all leads: ₹67.68 crore

### Business Insights
- IndiaMART generated the highest number of leads and the highest revenue among the lead sources analysed.
- Paints & Coatings had a 15.17% win rate.
- Molecular Sieve 4A had the highest win rate among the three products.
- Price Too High was the most frequently recorded reason for lost deals.
